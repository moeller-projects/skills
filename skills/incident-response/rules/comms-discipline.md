# Rule: Comms Discipline

> Impact: high

## Description

Communicate facts on a schedule: what is impacted, what is being done, when the next update lands. Speculation and blame corrode trust faster than the outage does.

## Apply When

- Any stakeholder-facing or customer-facing incident communication.

## Checks

- Every update states impact, current action, and next update time.
- Suspected causes are labeled as suspected or omitted.
- No person or team is named as responsible in incident comms.
- External communications get explicit approval before publishing.

## Anti-Pattern

"The database team probably broke something" posted to the status page while customers are refreshing it.
