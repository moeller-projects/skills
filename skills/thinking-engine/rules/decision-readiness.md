# Rule: Decision Readiness

> Impact: high

## Description

End with a recommendation that is specific enough to decide on, including the reason it wins and what should happen next.

## Apply When

- The user needs a plan, recommendation, or critique output.

## Checks

- MUST state the recommended path and why it is preferred now.
- MUST include concrete next steps or questions needed to unblock action.

## Anti-Pattern

Ending with broad observations that leave the user unsure what to do next.
