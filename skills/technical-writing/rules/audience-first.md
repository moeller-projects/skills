# Rule: Audience First

> Impact: critical

## Description

Optimize the document for the reader's task, context, and likely questions rather than for author convenience.

## Apply When

- Creating or revising technical documentation.

## Checks

- The intended audience is named or obvious from the structure.
- Content answers what the reader must do next.
- The reader's current need is named in mode terms: are they learning (tutorial), performing a known task (how-to), looking up a fact (reference), or trying to understand (explanation)? See `rules/diataxis-mode.md`.

## Anti-Pattern

Mixing beginner, operator, and maintainer guidance into one undifferentiated document.
