# Rule: Comment Quality

> Impact: high

## Description

Every finding must be specific, actionable, and written for the author. A finding the author cannot act on is noise.

## Apply When

- Writing any review finding.

## Checks

- Each finding names file, line, issue, why it matters, and a concrete suggestion.
- Questions are phrased as questions, not disguised defects.
- Nits are labeled nit and never block a verdict.
- Tone addresses the code, never the author.

## Anti-Pattern

"This looks wrong" with no line reference, no reason, and no suggested change.
